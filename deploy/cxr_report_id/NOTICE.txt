models/chexone/  -  CheXOne 3B + LoRA
==============================================================================================

Base model: CheXOne (StanfordAIMI/CheXOne, https://huggingface.co/StanfordAIMI/CheXOne),
Stanford AIMI. Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00493
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Changes made: the files in models/chexone/ are a LoRA adapter trained on top of CheXOne to
write chest X-ray report sections in Bahasa Indonesia (Indonesian translations of CheXpert
Plus and MIMIC-CXR reports), together with CheXOne's tokenizer and processor files, which were
re-saved by the training tools (Unsloth / TRL) and may differ from the originals. The adapter
does not contain CheXOne's weights; they are downloaded from Hugging Face under CheXOne's
license. The adapter is shared under the same CC BY-NC 4.0 terms.

NON-COMMERCIAL USE ONLY. CheXOne is itself post-trained from Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct
(https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct); that model's license terms apply as well.
